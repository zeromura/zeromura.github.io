NOME_DO_APP

NOME_DO_APP is a personal tool that prepares short video clips and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner connects their own TikTok account once, using TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The tool creates short video clips on the owner's computer.
  3. Using TikTok's Content Posting API, the tool sends each clip to the owner's TikTok account, where the owner reviews and publishes it.

This tool is for personal use only. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other people.
